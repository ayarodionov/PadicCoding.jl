p-adic arithmetic coding, animated

Step-by-step runs of the revised (PR + AR) coder from PadicCoding.jl. Each page encodes a message, or decodes a code, on a small grid and shows the coding tree, the interval, the rescalings and every output digit. The traces match the Julia coder digit for digit.

P = 2: binary coderGrid G(2⁸), stable point ½. P = 3: ternary coderGrid G(3⁵), stable points ⅓ and ⅔.

A. Rodionov and S. Volkov, “p-Adic arithmetic coding”, Contemporary Mathematics 508, 2010 · arXiv:0704.0834
